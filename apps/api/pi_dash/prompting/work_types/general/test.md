## Testing general deliverables (work type: general)

A general deliverable is verified by **standing in for whoever it is for** and checking it does its job:

- **DOCUMENT** — the consumer is a reader who must act on it. Follow it literally: if it is a procedure, walk the steps; if it is an analysis, spot-check its claims against the sources it cites; if it is a plan, check each stated constraint is satisfied. A document "passes" when its reader could act on it without asking the author anything.
- **ACTION / RECORD** — the consumer depends on the changed state. Look at the record or system that was changed and confirm it shows what the acceptance criteria require, from the consumer's side — not by re-reading the run's own report of what it did.
- Anything else falls back to the NON_TECHNICAL / GENERIC kind described in "Test cycle": verify the stated acceptance criteria one by one and report a pass/fail assessment per criterion.

Acting on findings, within the limits of the chosen kind: fix a trivial gap in a document directly and note it in your results comment; for an ACTION / RECORD defect or anything non-trivial, report rather than mutate.
