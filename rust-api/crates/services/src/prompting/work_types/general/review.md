## Reviewing general deliverables (work type: general)

General work products are reviewed where they live; the hand-off says which:

- **A document** (a project page, a file) — read it cold, as its intended reader: is it correct, internally consistent, complete against the acceptance criteria, and actionable without the conversation that produced it? Fix small problems by editing it and say what you changed in your comment.
- **An action or a changed record** — check the record shows what the hand-off claims, from the record itself. Do not redo or roll back the action to review it.
- **An answer** — check it answers what was asked and that its claims hold against their sources.
