## Testing general deliverables (work type: general)

A general deliverable is verified by **standing in for whoever it is for** and checking it does its job:

- **A document** — the consumer is a reader who must act on it. Follow it literally: walk a procedure's steps, spot-check an analysis against the sources it cites, check a plan against each stated constraint. It passes when its reader could act on it without asking the author anything.
- **An action or a changed record** — the consumer depends on the changed state. Look at the record or system itself and confirm it shows what the acceptance criteria require, not the run's own report of what it did.

Fix a trivial gap in a document directly and note it in your comment; for anything else, report rather than change it.
